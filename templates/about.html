ABOUT PATHFINDER

A little clarity for your next step.

Pathfinder is a student-focused resume feedback tool. It helps you surface skills, explore role alignment, and decide what to improve next.

How your data is handled

  • Uploaded files are processed in memory and aren't written to disk. A separate copy of the full pasted or extracted resume text isn't saved.
  • The structured analysis, source filename, selected role, timestamp, and analysis mode are saved in the configured database. Extracted section details may repeat information from your resume.
  • If Gemini is configured, resume text is sent to Google's Gemini API for analysis. Configure this only after reviewing the provider's privacy terms.
  • This starter app has no user accounts or access controls. Use it only on a trusted local machine until authentication and deployment safeguards are added.

How to read the results

Role-match estimates use a disclosed rubric: required-skill coverage contributes 70%, and project evidence, experience evidence, and education or certification evidence contribute 10% each. These percentages are not hiring probabilities or ATS scores. AI-generated suggestions may need human review; no result guarantees interviews or hiring outcomes.
